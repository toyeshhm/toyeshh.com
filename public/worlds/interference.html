Work, 2025 to now

Four rooms of work, one painting each. Every painting is drawn from the real numbers on its label: one wave cycle per month on the job, and a fold where the result changes the line.

Wins

One line per team in the field. At this density the lines blur into grey and you can only find the one drawn in full black.

Projects

Each piece lays one wave grating per tool in its medium. Two tools, two gratings. Where they cross, the moiré is the work. Hold the pointer over a piece to press into it.

Catalogue

Every painting on this page is drawn live in Canvas 2D with plain JavaScript, no libraries. Frames only run while a painting is on screen. With reduced motion on, they hang still.
